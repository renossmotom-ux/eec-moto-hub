COMPANY PROFILE

Our company offers variety of products which can meet your multifarious demands. We adhere to the management principles of "quality first, customer first and credit-based" since the establishment of the company and always do our best to satisfy potential needs of our customers. Our company is sincerely willing to cooperate with enterprises from all over the world in order to realize a win-win situation since the trend of economic globalization has developed with anirresistible force.

Yueqing Yabiya Motorcycle Accessories Co., Ltd. (formerly known as Yueqing Hongzhou Motorcycle Accessories Co., Ltd. ) was restructured and established in 2015. With a factory area of 12, 000 square meters, the company specializes in the research, development, production, and sales of riding equipment such as safety helmets, tail boxes, and gloves.

The company holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 certifications and has obtained various product certifications including CCC, DOT, and ECE, along with design patents and copyright certificates. It owns proprietary brands such as "BYB/Yabiya" and "RNG". Equipped with advanced production facilities and a professional technical team, the company has accumulated extensive expertise in helmet design and manufacturing.

Since its inception, the company has adhered to the principle of "customer first, quality as the foundation". Leveraging domestic e-commerce platforms (e. g., Tmall, Pinduoduo, JD. COM, Douyin) and its technological strengths, it has collaborated with renowned partners such as Shanghai Forever Bicycle, Yadea Electric Vehicle Group, PEAK, and Watsons LINE FRIENDS, securing a competitive edge in the market and achieving rapid, stable growth. In international markets, its core business focuses on OEM and ODM projects in the Asia-Pacific region and Central and South America.

Guided by the philosophy of "innovation, rigor, and service", Yabiya Motorcycle Accessories is committed to technological advancement and product development, continuously delivering cutting-edge solutions and exceptional service to its clients.
