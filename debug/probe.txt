The success of recent editions, marked by record-breaking numbers of visitors, qualified professionals, media, and stakeholders, confirms the strength of a format that is both current and closely aligned with market needs. Looking ahead to EICMA 2026 (3–8 November) , the organization is further strengthening its commitment to enhancing the exhibition experience, continuing to invest in advanced services and networking tools designed to maximize exhibitors’ return on investment.
