LUYUAN Group Holdings Limited (02451. HK), founded in 1997, is one of the pioneering brands of electric two-wheelers in China and listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in October 2023.

The Group has always LED technological innovation and standard-setting in the industry, with over 1, 300 patents applied for in total. It is the only company in the industry to participate in all three national standard formulations. As the global inventor of liquid-cooled motors, it has won the First Prize for Scientific and Technological Progress and the First Prize for Scientific and Technological Invention from the CNLIC.

The Group has 4 smart manufacturing bases worldwide, with an annual planned production capacity of 10 million units. Its products are exported to more than 80 countries and regions, with over 14, 000 stores serving more than 30 million users globally.

Centered on two-wheel and three-wheel electric vehicles, the Group covers business ecosystems such as commercial use and battery rental-swapping. Integrating R&D, manufacturing, marketing and services, it is committed to creating all-scenario active-mobility solutions. Meanwhile, it is actively laying out cutting-edge ecosystems like embodied robots to promote technological ecologization and industrial intelligent transformation.
