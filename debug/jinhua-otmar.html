COMPANY PROFILE

Jinhua Otmar Technology Co., Ltd is a professional electric bicycle manufacturer in Jinhua City, Zhejiang Province, China. Our primary business is the production of electric bikes, electric trikes, and compatible e-bike trailers with CE, UKCA, UL and ISO certification.

We have more 17 years of export experience and R&D experience and export to about 48 other countries including the USA, Canada, Austrlia, New Zealand, UK and Europe and so on. We're always trying to be at least a step ahead of the competition while always looking for new ways to improve our products.

Before we ship anything to you it goes through a meticulous rigorous testing process. After that the product is given our stamp of approval and then subsequently shipped to you.
