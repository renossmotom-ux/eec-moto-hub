About Tao Motor
Founded in 1985, located in Jinyun County, Zhejiang Province. We are near to Jinhua-Lishui-Wenzhou Expressway and are adjacent to China Hardware City - Yongkang. ZHE JIANG TAOTAO VEHICLES Co., Ltd has been a comprehensive large group enterprise integrating the R&D, production and sale, covering an area of 20, 000 square meters. We have 2, 000 employees and over 200 staff members with intermediate & senior technical titlesOur main products are ATVs, motorcycles and electric vehicles. All of our products have gained good reputations from domestic and overseas markets for years. 'Tao Motor' is wellknow brand in Domestic and Overseas Market.
R&D Capability
Tao Motor has a great R&D department which covers more than 400 square meters, and has more than 200 staff members with senior & intermediate technical titles. We are ready to provide you with OEM and ODM services. With our joint efforts, Taotao products have been CE, EEC, Reach, RoHS, EPA, CARB and ISO9001: 2000 certified. Taotao develops 5-10 new products every year to meet different requirements from different markets.
Export Markets
Tao Motor has sevral different self Owned sales company in different countries. Annual sales volume is more than 120 million dollars. TaoMotor products have been exported to the USA, Canada, Europe, South America, the Middle East and Southeast Asia.
Large Output
TaoMotor is a first-grade production enterprise in China's motorcycle, electric vehicle and engine fields, and is the first corporation of China and the third corporation of world in aspect of attaining CPSC certificate from America. Our group has 4 casting shops, 4 welding shops, 2 mould shops, 1 injection shop, 1 hub-making shop and 1 heat-coping shop, and 16 production assembly lines for different products, as well as 4 plastic-spraying lines and 4 paint-coating lines. Our group has invested over 10 million Yuan in establishing a testing center for motorcycles, electric vehicles and related spare parts, more than 0.1 billion Yuan in production equipment and over 20 million Yuan in testing equipment or instruments. Our first-class production facilities, advanced testing equipment and skilled workers allow TaoMotor to produce 300, 000 motorcycles, 300, 000 engines, 300, 000 ATVs/Go Karts, 200, 000 Motorcycles, 300, 000 electric vehicles every year.
